The public release is near. Before that, the latest update gives your library what it was missing: a life beyond your device.

![The "Read on my other devices" card in the app](/blog/images/sync-read-anywhere.png)

## A safety copy, first

Create your account: an encrypted copy of your library follows you. Phone lost, replaced, reset? The secret phrase generated from the app's settings is all you need to get everything back: books, shelves, contacts, loans. Nobody but you can read that copy.

![The Backup and recovery section in the settings](/blog/images/sync-backup-settings.png)

## All your devices, next

The same library on your phone, your tablet, your computer, merged automatically. It is ready, and it switches on with the latest update.

![The account screen: authorized devices, sync and add-device actions](/blog/images/sync-devices.png)

## The whole household, tomorrow

One shared library at home, where everyone finds the books on their own device and sees who published what. That is the direction.

## Your move

A few more days of testing, then publication on Apple's App Store and the application to Google's Play Store. Update the app (every version carries the latest fixes), create your account, and tell us about the slightest hiccup at [contact@bibliogenius.org](mailto:contact@bibliogenius.org). Every report counts in this final stretch.

Thank you for being part of the adventure. See you very soon!
