Next to the contacts you pair one by one, BiblioGenius offers a directory: libraries that chose to be visible, which you can discover and follow without ever meeting them.

## Making yourself visible

In **Settings > Online Sharing**, turn on **"Make my library visible to other libraries"**. You then appear in the directory, with your library name and the books you share.

Two safeguards:

- **"Require approval for followers"**: new followers must be approved before they reach your shared books. Their requests wait for you in the requests tab.
- Your city stays a local preference: it is only published if you choose to share it. It then serves to surface the libraries near you.

Nothing is published until you turn that sharing on.

## Discovering and following

In **Network > Explore**, browse the published libraries, filter by location, then follow the ones you like. Following gives you access to their shared catalogue and lets you ask them for a loan.

If nobody near you has shared their city yet, the screen invites you to be the first: that is what gets local discovery started.

## Follow or pair?

Both coexist and do different things.

- **Following** goes through the online directory. It is immediate, remote, and enough to browse a catalogue and request a loan.
- **[Pairing](connect-friends.html)** creates a direct encrypted link between your two devices, by QR code or invitation link. That link is what enables peer-to-peer exchanges, including over your local Wi-Fi network.

A library paired on another of your devices shows up as "known" but not paired here: pairing is done device by device, see [Encrypted account and multiple devices](account-sync.html).
