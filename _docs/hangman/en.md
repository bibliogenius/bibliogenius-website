Hangman uses the titles of your own library as its board: your job is to find the hidden title, letter by letter.

## Starting a game

The game lives in the Games section. If it is not there, enable it in the settings: games are optional modules, each one switched on or off separately.

## How it works

A title from your library is drawn at random and masked. You call out letters: the right ones fall into place, the wrong ones advance the drawing. Finish the word before the drawing finishes you.

Since the titles come from your own books, the difficulty follows your library: the more it grows, the more the games renew themselves.
