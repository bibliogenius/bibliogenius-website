BiblioGenius ships ready-made lists: literary prizes, classics, genres, manga, youth titles, back-to-school reading. They are there to start a library, fill a shelf, or simply see what you are missing.

## Where to find them

From your library or from your collections, open **"Discover"** to browse the lists. They are grouped by category, and the ones in your reading languages come first.

## Importing a list

Pick the list, then decide which status the imported books get:

- **Owned**, if you already have those books at home;
- **To read**, to build a pile;
- **Wishlist**, for the books you are still after.

The import creates a collection named after the list, and BiblioGenius fills in each book's metadata (cover, summary, publisher) along the way.

## What your network adds

If you import a list onto your wishlist, the app tells you how many of those books are already available in your network, and notifies you in one grouped message: "3 book(s) from 'Booker Prize' available in your network". See [My wishlist](wishlist.html).

## Back-to-school lists

The "Back to school" category follows the French curriculum: CM1-CM2, entering 6e, entering 5e. Each list is written for its own level and updated as the curriculum changes, so a level only appears once it is genuinely covered.
