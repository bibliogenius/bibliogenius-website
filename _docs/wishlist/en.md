A book you want to read but do not own belongs in BiblioGenius: put it on your wishlist. It stays in your library, marked as wanted, and your network can then help you find it.

## Putting a book on the wishlist

Add the book like any other, by scanning, searching or by hand, then give it the **"Wishlist"** status (the heart). You can also do it from a ready-made list at import time.

A wanted book does not count as owned: it does not show up as available to your contacts, and the CSV export marks it "Wanted".

## Seeing who owns a book you are after

On the details page of a wanted book, an **"Available from"** card appears when a library in your network owns it. You can send a borrow request straight from that card.

The card stays invisible while there is no match: these encounters are rare, they depend on the overlap between your libraries, not on a flaw in the app.

## Knowing who is after your books

On the details page of a book you own, a **"Wanted by"** card lists the libraries whose wishlist contains that title, with a **"Lend"** button to offer the loan in one gesture.

Lending stays a free, one-sided gesture: nobody is told that you saw their search, and nothing commits you. If you happen to be after a book that person owns, a discreet note tells you so, without turning the exchange into an obligation.

## Notifications

When a book on your wishlist becomes available in your network, a notification tells you. On a ready-made list import the notification is grouped: "3 books from 'Booker Prize' available in your network".

## Privacy

Matches only ever cover books that are actually shared. A book marked private never shows up, neither in "Available from" nor in "Wanted by".
